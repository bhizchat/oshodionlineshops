Our Mission

Oshodi Market has always been known for variety, value, and hustle. Our mission is simple: make it just as easy to browse and find what you need from Oshodi online as it is to walk the market in person — without the noise and the long walk between stalls.

What We Do

  • List real shops and products across fabrics, clothing, electronics, foodstuffs, accessories, and many more categories.
  • Make it easy to search and browse by category, so buyers can quickly find what they're looking for.
  • Let shoppers leave genuine reviews and ratings to help others shop with confidence.
  • Connect buyers directly to vendors, keeping the local, personal feel of shopping in Oshodi Market.

Who We Are

Oshodi Market Online is built and powered by StrategicX Ltd, a technology company focused on bringing Nigerian local markets online and helping small businesses reach more customers.
